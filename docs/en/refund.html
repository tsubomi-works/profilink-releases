Refunds

Try it free for 14 days first

Profilink is fully free for 14 days before you buy, with nothing held back. You only pay after that. Please use this time to confirm that links route correctly with your browsers and profiles.

Refunds after purchase

Because the free trial lets you check everything first, we do not normally offer refunds after purchase. Please confirm during the trial that the app works on your Mac.

If something does not work

Please get in touch first. Most problems are solved in settings (setting the default browser again, reviewing your rules, reloading profiles). If not, once we know the cause we will fix the app. Write to info@tsubomi.jp.

Exceptions

Purchases are handled by a reseller acting as merchant of record. Where their policy or the law of your country gives you a right to a refund, we follow it, and the refund goes through them.
