Privacy Policy

Profilink does not collect your personal information. Here are the details.

What the app does not collect

  • Personal details such as your name or email address
  • The URLs you open, your browsing history, or usage statistics
  • Anything inside your browsers (page contents, cookies, passwords)

All routing happens on your Mac. The links you open are never sent anywhere.

What the app reads

  • Your browser profiles — the profile names, folder names, signed-in email addresses and pictures in the Local State file of Chrome, Edge and Brave. They are shown in the chooser and in Settings only, and never sent anywhere.
  • The app a link came from — macOS tells Profilink which app (Slack, Mail and so on) handed it the link. It is used only for source-app rules and the history, and never sent anywhere.

When the app goes online

The app connects to the internet for one thing only:

  • Checking for updates — once a day it reads the list of the latest releases on GitHub (github.com) to see whether a new version is out. This is an ordinary HTTP request with nothing that identifies you. You can turn it off in Settings.

What is stored on your Mac

  • Settings — your rules, which profiles appear in the chooser, and so on, in ~/Library/Application Support/Profilink
  • History — the URL of each link received, the app it came from, where it opened and when, up to the latest 500 entries, in ~/Library/Application Support/Profilink. You can clear it or turn it off at any time in Settings › History.
  • Your license key and trial start date — in the macOS keychain

All of it stays on your Mac.

When you buy

Payment is processed by a reseller. They handle your card details; we never receive them, and their privacy policy applies. We learn only what is needed to issue your license.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub keeps access logs (including IP addresses). We use no analytics, ads or cookies.
